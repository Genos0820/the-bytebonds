Kaale Coat Wale

A legal-tech platform pairing legal education with corporate advisory — company registration, trademarks and drafting.

Visit live site

The challenge

The brand began as a student-focused legal initiative and grew into corporate advisory. The site had to speak to both audiences — learners and founders — without feeling like two businesses stitched together.

What we built

We organised the site around clear service pages for company registration, trademarks and drafting, kept a blog for legal explainers, and gave the Academy its own section — one coherent brand with a clear path for each type of visitor.

Why we built it this way

Two audiences who want opposite things. A law student is browsing and will read three articles before deciding whether to trust you. A founder who needs a company registered wants the service, the scope and a way to start, and resents every paragraph standing between them and it. The tempting fix is one averaged tone that serves neither. We gave each its own route in from the home page instead — the Academy is a section with its own pace, and the advisory services are direct about what they are and what you get. One brand, two speeds.
